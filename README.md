# Order of Aurelia
an unciv mod

Order of Aurelia is an extension ruleset for **Civ V - Gods & Kings**, centered on King Lucian and a civilization built around strong social cohesion, professional institutions, scientific development, and defensive military organization.

## Civilization
**Order of Aurelia** — Capital: Aurelia — Leader: King Lucian

**Many Hands, One Will:** +50% Production while the empire is Happy.

**A Future Worth Building:** completing a building grants Science and Culture equal to 50% of its base Production cost. The implementation represents this with construction triggers for the base Gods & Kings buildings and rounds odd costs down.

## Religion
**Cult of Aurelia**
- **The Ever-Burning Hearth:** +20% growth.
- **The Call of Aurelia:** +25% Production toward Military units while at war.

## Institutions
- **Athena's University** replaces University: +2 Science, +25% Science.
- **Hephaestus's Workshop** replaces Workshop: +2 Production, +25% Production.
- **Hermes's Guild** replaces Market: +2 Gold, +25% Gold.
- **Asclepius's Palace** replaces Hospital: +2 Food, +25% Food, +1 Happiness.
- **Eyes of Heimdall** is a Renaissance-era National Wonder granting 3 Spies and +50% Spy Effectiveness.
- **The Aurelia Project** is a Future Tech World Wonder providing +100% Aluminum resource production and +100% Uranium resource production.

## Military
- **The Order's Knights** replaces Swordsman: +10% Strength, +10% Production cost.
- **The Order's Riflemen** replaces Rifleman: +10% Strength, +10% Production cost.
- **The Order's Infantry** replaces Infantry: +10% Strength, +15% Production cost.
- **The Order's Neo-Infantry** replaces Mechanized Infantry: +10% Strength, +1 Movement, +20% Production cost.
- **Hannibal's Legacy** replaces Great General and provides a +15% Strength aura to Military units within 2 tiles.

## Deferred mechanics
The current Unciv JSON unique system does not expose a generic repeating every-5-turns trigger or a generic unit-count cap, so Hannibal's requested recurring spawn and 3-unit maximum are not implemented yet. The requested +10% defense specifically against ranged attacks likewise has no current documented unique. Custom religion artwork is deferred.
